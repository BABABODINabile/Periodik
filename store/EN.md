# Périodik — store copy (EN)

**Name**: Périodik

**Short description (80 chars)**
3D periodic table, ions, quiz and chemistry tools for studying.

**Full description**
Périodik brings together everything you need to study chemistry, from high school to university:
• An interactive periodic table of all 118 elements, with 2D and 3D views, search and category filters.
• A detailed sheet for every element: atomic structure, properties, uses, hazards and STSE context, exportable as a high-resolution image.
• An ion engine with 132 species and a detail sheet per ion (charge, official equation, level).
• Tools: equation balancer, molar mass (hydrates included), empirical formula and solubility table.
• A dynamic quiz across 7 topics, with custom length and topics, plus instant explanations.
Bilingual FR/EN interface, light/dark/system theme, fully optimised for mobile.

**What's new in 1.0**
Initial release: interactive table, 3D atom and Bohr diagram, 132 ions, 4 tools, dynamic quiz, image export, dark mode and bilingual support.
